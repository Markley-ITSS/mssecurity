What we do

Security systems, installed by people who care about the finish.

From a single doorbell camera to a full alarm and CCTV package, every job is planned, installed and set up properly — cables hidden, app configured, and you shown exactly how it all works.

Intruder alarms

Wireless and wired intruder alarm systems with app control and instant notifications — arm, disarm and check your home from anywhere.

CCTV camera installation

Professional 4K camera systems with full-colour night vision, wide-angle and PTZ options. See faces and number plates, day or night.

Video doorbells

See and speak to whoever's at the door from your phone, with clear two-way audio and recorded footage of every visit.

Access control

Keypad, fob and app-based entry systems for homes and businesses — control who comes and goes, and when.

4G & WiFi cameras

No broadband? No problem. 4G and WiFi PTZ cameras with 20x zoom for sites, yards, farms and remote properties.

Upgrades & maintenance

Old, tired cameras replaced with modern kit. Repairs, health-checks and system upgrades to keep everything running.
