Community Involvement

More than security — supporting our community.

The North East is where we live, work and raise our families, so giving something back is important to us. MS Security Systems is proud to sponsor local grassroots football team Rutherford Under 13 Swifts — after an amazing season and a well-earned promotion, we're looking forward to supporting the team throughout the 2026–27 season. We're also proud to support the Kibblesworth Time Capsule, helping preserve local stories, memories and community history for future generations.

Why we get involved

Local business, local roots.

Grassroots clubs are at the heart of our towns and villages. They give young people somewhere to belong, help keep them active and bring families and communities together every weekend. As a family-run North East business, we're proud to support the people, teams and community projects that make our area such a great place to live and work.

Team, kit and community sponsorship

From match shirts and training gear to local events and community projects, we're proud to support the people and organisations that bring our area together. We help local teams take to the pitch looking the part while also backing events, initiatives and projects that make a positive difference across the North East — all proudly carrying the MS Security name.

Rooted in the North East

Everything we support is close to home — across the same Tyneside, Durham and Northumberland communities where we live, work and help protect homes and businesses every day.
