What we do

Security systems installed with care, attention to detail and a professional finish.

From a single video doorbell to a complete alarm and CCTV system, every installation is carefully planned, neatly fitted and fully set up. We hide cables wherever possible, configure your app and show you exactly how everything works before we leave.

Intruder alarms

Professionally installed wired and wireless alarm systems with app control and instant alerts, allowing you to arm, disarm and check the status of your property from anywhere in the world.

CCTV System Installation

Professionally installed CCTV systems with full-colour night vision and excellent, crisp coverage using AI. Capture clear, detailed footage of people, vehicles and activity around your property, day and night.

Video Doorbells

See and speak to whoever is at your door from your phone, with clear two-way audio and HD recorded footage of every visit — with no monthly fees or subscription charges, ever.

Remote CCTV Solutions

We install CCTV in remote buildings and locations without internet access, using reliable 4G connections or microwave links. Ideal for farms, yards, outbuildings, construction sites and other hard-to-reach properties.

Upgrades & Maintenance

Keep your CCTV and alarm systems working reliably with professional repairs, servicing and upgrades. We replace outdated cameras, carry out system health checks, change alarm batteries and install additional sounders, keypads, PIR sensors and other equipment as required. Our no fix, no fee policy means you won't pay a repair charge unless we get your system working again.

Modern alarm systems, professionally upgraded or taken over.

Old alarm systems can become unreliable, difficult to use and expensive to keep repairing. We can upgrade outdated panels, keypads, sensors and external sounders, or fully take over an existing system and bring it back into reliable working order. Where possible, we retain suitable existing equipment to keep costs down — replacing only what's needed and reconfiguring the system properly.
